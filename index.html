Singapore Housing Explorer ↗

An interactive map of Singapore shaded by median resale price for each flat type. Scrub or play the timeline from 1993 to 2030, and select a region to see its price index, price-to-income ratio, population and the policy changes of that period. Prices after today come from a regression model, with a variance of 5% a year.

NBA Season Projections ↗

Projects every team's 2026–27 record and net rating with ridge regression on nba_api data. Each roster box shows a team's top six players by expected minutes, drawn as shapes by role, so you can see at a glance how well a roster fits together. The predictions were comparable to ESPN's power rankings.

Flat-type price history (S$ thousand)

Source: HDB resale flat transactions (data.gov.sg, Jan 1990 to Oct 2026). Each value is the median of all sales in the region and year, pooled across towns so larger towns weigh more; cells with fewer than 5 sales are blank. Files up to Feb 2012 are by approval date, later ones by registration date, and 2026 covers January to October. Medians are not quality-adjusted: flat age and size mix shift over time.

Method: yearly log change in the resale index is regressed on income growth, population growth, last year’s price-to-income gap (mean reversion, constrained negative) and a cooling-policy dummy (lagged one year, since measures follow booms), then rolled forward under your scenario. Land follows the resale index. The band reflects residual noise only. With about 25 approximate observations, treat this as a sandbox, not a prediction.

Data tables

Italic rows are estimates or projections. The selected year is highlighted.
Data caveat: household income is SingStat (resident employed households, excl. employer CPF; 2010+ mean is estimated as 1.39× the median; 2024+ income is estimated). Land and population series are approximations. The national price index and all regional prices come from HDB resale transactions (index = national 4-room median, 2009 = 100). Region shapes are URA Master Plan 2019 planning areas, simplified and grouped into 12 regions, and regional prices are real medians of HDB resale transactions for 1990–2026, and later years are scaled by the model’s national path. Years after 2026 come from the scenario model above and are not a forecast or advice. Replace the data tables at the top of the script with real figures for rigorous work.

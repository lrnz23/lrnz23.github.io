Method: yearly log change in the resale index is regressed on income growth, population growth, last year’s price-to-income gap (mean reversion, constrained negative) and a cooling-policy dummy (lagged one year, since measures follow booms), then rolled forward under your scenario. Land follows the resale index. The band reflects residual noise only. With about 25 approximate observations, treat this as a sandbox, not a prediction.

Italic rows are estimates or projections. The selected year is highlighted.
Data caveat: household income is SingStat (resident employed households, excl. employer CPF; 2010+ mean is estimated as 1.39× the median; 2024+ income is estimated). Price, land and population series are approximations built from public HDB, URA and SingStat trends, not official figures. Regions are simplified shapes, and regional prices are modelled as multiples of the national index. Years after 2026 come from the scenario model above and are not a forecast or advice.
